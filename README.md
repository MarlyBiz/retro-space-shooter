# Retro Space Shooter

Mobile-first arcade shooter (v2.2). Levels advance by **distance flown** (short segments) — each level starts a bit faster than the last, then ramps harder by the end, with a big LEVEL banner. The Tank is a twin-gun fighter with a slower double-shot. Defend your homeland space station: enemies that slip past drain HOMELAND POWER; at zero you lose a life. Collisions and enemy fire also cost a life. Distance and speed ramp up; new enemy types appear as you fly farther (Diver, Weaver 250m, Zig-Zag 600m, Dart 1400m, Swooper 1500m, Tank 2200m).

**Play:** https://marlybiz.github.io/retro-space-shooter/

- Touch/drag to move (including on the base strip). Auto-fire. Desktop: arrow keys / A D or mouse.
- Power-ups: Twin, Spread, Rapid, Laser, Bomb, Repair, 1-Up.
- Best score and distance saved locally. Installable PWA with Share button.
